Where To?

Where To? was a web platform and business idea created to make events in Maastricht, Netherlands, easier to discover. The goal was to connect university students, local residents, event organisers, and venues through one central hub.

I worked alongside my partner to plan the concept, shape the platform’s features, contact event organisers and venues, and help run the project. We wanted to create more than an event listing website: we wanted to build a community around Maastricht’s events and give organisers a dedicated space to promote their work.

Key Features

  • Homepage highlighting upcoming events and local activities.
  • Event directory with categories including House, Techno, Pop, R&B, and other events.
  • Dedicated event pages with descriptions, dates, images, organiser information, social links, tags, and galleries.
  • Organiser and venue directory to help users discover the people and places behind Maastricht’s events.
  • Dedicated profile pages for event organisers, giving them space to tell their story and promote their brand.
  • Threaded comment sections where visitors could share thoughts, connect with others, and reply to existing comments.
  • Optional social media links attached to user comments.
  • Contact form for users and event organisers to get in touch with the team.
  • Responsive layout designed for both desktop and mobile users.

My Contribution

I contributed to the planning, design, development, and business side of the project. This included discussing the platform’s purpose and features, building and adapting website pages, creating the event and organiser experiences, developing the comment functionality, and reaching out to event organisers and venues.

Because I was based in Bristol while my partner was connected to Maastricht, keeping the event listings continuously updated became increasingly difficult. When my friend later left the university, maintaining relationships with local organisers and keeping the platform current became impractical, so the project was eventually discontinued. However, it gave me valuable experience in full-stack development, product planning, stakeholder communication, community-focused design, and working on a real business idea from concept to launch.
